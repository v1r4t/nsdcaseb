01 / About

01 / About

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham

Across data, AI and emerging technology — from first principles to shipped systems.

The National Student Data Corps at Amrita School of Engineering, Bengaluru is a student chapter of the NEBDHub's NSDC initiative — a community programme that gives students resources and opportunities to learn data science in a community of support.

Our mission is to foster innovation, collaboration and professional growth while addressing real-world challenges: workshops, hackathons and industry sessions that connect students with practitioners.

Sessions

Weekly, 90 minutes, student-led. Papers, tooling, and build reviews.

Builds

Small teams ship one real system per semester, in public.

Research

A reading group for evals, retrieval and applied ML on campus data.

09 / FAQ

09 / FAQ

Before you apply.

Four answers

01How do I join?+

Applications open at the start of each semester. When recruitment is open, the form is linked from the Now section; it asks for your year, branch, and two short answers.

02Who is eligible?+

1st to 3rd-year students at Amrita Bengaluru, any branch. Use your @bl.students.amrita.edu email — applications from other domains are rejected.

03How much time does it take?+

One 90-minute session most Saturdays, plus your share of a project. Hackathon weeks run longer.

04What happens after I apply?+

The core team reviews every application and emails a decision. Status is pending, selected or not selected — you are told either way, and you can apply again next cycle.
